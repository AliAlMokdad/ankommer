ANKOMMER is an independent guide that organizes public information, built for people who have already moved to Denmark — or are about to, through legal channels. Always verify important information with official Danish authorities.

Privacy & your data

ANKOMMER stores your language, theme, completed tasks, Viking Points, and (optional) wizard answers locally in your browser — never sent to a server. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking. The AI agents on this site only gather and organize public data. They never collect, store, or share your personal data with anyone. Calculator, weather, and exchange-rate lookups call public APIs (Open-Meteo, exchange-rate API, Rejseplanen, DAWA, nyidanmark.dk links) which receive only the request data needed to answer. To clear everything, use your browser's clear-site-data tool, or the "Download my plan" / "Restore from file" buttons above.
